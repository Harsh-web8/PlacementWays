# Competing with PrepInsta: roadmap

PrepInsta wins on volume of free content, search rankings and years of trust. A UI redesign does not close that gap. These are the levers, in priority order.

## 1. Content (the real moat, needs people, not code)
- Publish a full guide for the 20 most-hired companies (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture, Cognizant, Capgemini, HCL, Tech Mahindra, Amazon, etc.): pattern, syllabus, eligibility, salary, interview rounds, with a visible "last verified" date and official sources. Accuracy is your edge; stale pages are their weakness.
- 30+ practice questions with explanations per topic, then full-length mocks per company pattern.
- Previous-year question pages per company and year.
- Blog: one useful article per week (interview experiences, "how to clear X in Y days").

## 2. Product features students will feel
- Free first mock with no signup (already built: guest mocks). Link it from every page.
- Topic-wise weak-area analytics after each test, and a "what to practise next" suggestion.
- Streaks and a daily practice question (retention).
- Shareable result card (growth).
- Mobile-first polish: most students are on phones.

## 3. SEO (how students actually find PrepInsta)
- One indexable page per company, topic and question set, each with unique text (generateMetadata exists; add structured data such as FAQ and Course).
- Keep the sitemap current and submit it in Google Search Console.
- Fast pages: test with PageSpeed Insights on a mid-range phone.

## 4. Trust and business
- Real testimonials and result numbers only once you have them. Do not show invented statistics.
- A clear free tier and honest pricing for premium mocks/courses.
- Working support email and WhatsApp/Telegram community channel.

## 5. Suggested order
1. Seed 10 company guides and 500 questions.
2. Soft launch to 2 or 3 colleges, collect feedback, fix.
3. Add streaks, weak-area analytics and share cards.
4. Start the weekly blog and submit to Search Console.

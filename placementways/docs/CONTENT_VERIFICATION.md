# Recruitment content intake and verification protocol (V1)

No student login, no student payments, and no student-facing AI chatbot or AI interviews are included. AI is used for private **draft assistance only**.

## 1. Evidence is required before publication

- Prefer company-owned official career listings, ATS records linked by the employer, and original official PDFs.
- Record the exact company, job role and hiring drive/year. Do not transfer the examination pattern from one role or cycle to another.
- Keep the original HTTPS source URL, the quoted passage supporting the fact, the date checked (with timezone), and an editor identifier.
- Record whether each source is official, secondary, candidate-reported, historical or unverified. Do not label a third-party blog as official.
- Do not infer precise deadlines, salary, eligibility cutoffs, number of questions or marking rules from a prior cycle.
- Never copy protected question banks or reproduce copyrighted pages. Create original preparation material or use content with clear permission.

## 2. Roles, drives and test patterns

For every role, seek: title, responsibilities, eligibility, salary, required skills, official source and review date.
For every drive, seek: hiring year or batch, application link, registration period, selection flow, source and check date.
For each round, seek: order, name, category, topics, number of questions, duration, marking and supporting source. Leave unspecified fields null.

**Public display:** a missing review date must say it is unavailable. When a record is more than 30 days old it is flagged as needing review; this does **not** prove an announcement is invalid. A job marked open with a past recorded close time is flagged instead of being advertised as definitely open.

## 3. Monitoring and approval

1. Add a specific allowed HTTPS hostname and register a source in the private admin dashboard.
2. Capture a source baseline and monitor subsequent changes (or extract explicit first-scan discoveries when supported).
3. Review AI-proposed facts against exact source excerpts. Mark conflicting or unsupported information for manual research.
4. Approve only changes that belong to the chosen company, role and drive.
5. Verify the public page after publication; audit logging and rollback exist to recover bad content.

## 4. Coverage is not the same as freshness

`/admin/recruitment/coverage` identifies structural gaps and stale reviews. It cannot guarantee source websites are available, that every university's private campus updates are captured, or that an external employer has not made an unannounced change. The editor should periodically inspect high-value sources manually.

## 5. Recommended release batches

Start with a manageable group of high-demand employers, validate complete guides for a few roles each, then scale after source failures and false-positive alerts are measured. Do not publish hundreds of automatically populated but unreviewed pages to hit a vanity metric.
